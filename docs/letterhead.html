Dear Jordan,

Thank you for hosting the squad on Saturday. Twenty-eight players, a dozen first-timers, and a room that stayed loud until the last of the fixtures came up on the screen — that is exactly the kind of evening that turns people who are curious about the club into people who turn up on a Tuesday.

Gotham Knights is New York City's LGBT rugby club. We field competitive sides in the Metropolitan union while keeping our doors open to players of every background and skill level, and we can only do that because businesses like yours give us somewhere to gather. Your support covered our post-match hospitality for the autumn season, and we'd like to recognize it properly: on the club website, in our match-day programs, and at our end-of-season dinner in December.

I'll follow up next week with a short summary of the season and a few photos you're welcome to use. In the meantime, if there is anything you need from us — a logo, a quote, a few players in kit for an event — just ask.

With thanks from the whole club,
