The focus — halftone, four directions

One dot grid. Four masks. Four moods.

Same printed-dot DNA from the Intro to Rugby flyer — only the fade changes. Fine and quiet for sponsors and decks; loud and gold for socials; coarse and graphic for posters; a spotlight to frame a face or a kit number.

A · Fine Fade

Quiet · decks, grants, sponsors

Fine Fade

A tight navy grid on paper, dissolving on the diagonal. The most professional read — texture you feel before you notice. The default for the Clubhouse register.

Pride Halftone
