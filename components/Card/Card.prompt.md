Content surface — pick `default` (paper, hairline + soft shadow), `outline` (2px navy), `navy` (dark panel), or `raised`.

```jsx
<Card featured>
  <h3>Tuesday Training</h3>
  <p>Wall Street · 8–9pm</p>
</Card>
```

`featured` paints the gold top-rule. Use one emphasis cue (border OR shadow), never both. Pairs with SectionHeading, Stat, Button inside.
