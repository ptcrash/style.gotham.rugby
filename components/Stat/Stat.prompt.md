Big mono figure + uppercase label for results, counts, and KPIs (founded year, members, wins).

```jsx
<Stat value="150+" label="Active members" />
<Stat value="1998" label="Established" accent="navy" align="center" />
```

Numbers are tabular mono (scoreboard feel). `accent`: `gold` (default), `navy`, `white` (use on dark). `align` left/center/right.
