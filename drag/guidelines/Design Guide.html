01 · The Register
A navy house with the spectrum lit on stage

The club ships two registers — Loud/Matchday (navy field, gold shouting) and Professional/Clubhouse (warm paper, navy ink). The drag show adds a third: the house lights going down. It is not a separate brand — the same club, in a third register. Technically it is one token file, tokens/drag.css, re-pointing the parent's semantic aliases under [data-theme="drag"]; every parent component rethemes without a fork.

✓ What carries over — non-negotiable
  • The crest and the wordmark. Untouched, unrecolored, no spectrum fills.
  • Navy as the anchor. The show is darker, not different.
  • Gold as the thread back to the club. The link and highlight color even though violet leads — the strongest signal this is the rugby club putting on a show.
  • The 4px spacing spine, 2px strokes, low athletic radii. Nothing gets bubbly.
  • The voice — bold, warm, a little cheeky; inclusive structurally, not decoratively.
→ What changes
  • The spectrum becomes structural. Frames, marquee rules, the lead accent — the one place the club's "Pride, not rainbow-everything" rule is deliberately relaxed. Still never wallpaper.
  • The house goes darker. --house-black (#04091a) sits below the club's darkest navy — a theater with the lights down, not a pitch at night.
  • Violet and pink ramps arrive. Defined locally; the parent has no violet scale.
  • The show title gets a marquee lockup. Harbour Bold, wordmark tracking — see Type.
We arecamp, deliberate, body-positive, cheeky — a rugby club in heels. Bad crops are craft: the parody-collage joke is deliberate and codified.
We are notsleazy, mean, or rainbow-wallpaper. Never punching down, never leaning on tired gay-bar clichés.
Usage. Link styles.css and set data-theme="drag" on <html> or any container — a single dark show panel can sit inside an otherwise Clubhouse-register page. The club's canonical system is never written to from here.

Dark house, three accents

✓ Do

Set the show title once, at poster scale, in Harbour Bold at wordmark tracking (--font-marquee / .gkd-marquee). A show title is a wordmark — it is the event's lockup.

✕ Don't

Use Harbour for headings, body, pull quotes, or a second element on the same surface. Harbour Decor is rejected — the blackletter cut failed legibility testing and is deliberately not bundled.

05 · Motion & Output Formats
Event and hold

Three output classes: print/graphics, venue screen (projector or LED wall, 16:9) and social video (9:16). Motion is authored as HTML/CSS and rendered to files with render/rec.cjs.

The design principle
  • Punctuation against stillness. The sheen crosses the crest in 16% of the loop, then sits still; the spark travels the rule once and waits. Continuous motion reads as a screensaver; event-and-hold reads as a stage.
  • --mo-event: 16% is the ceiling for a punctuating gesture — past ~20% it stops reading as an event.
  • One sanctioned exception: gkd-house-swell — a slow warm swell reads as a room, not a widget.
The hard constraints
  • If it moves, it is a CSS @keyframes rule. The renderer drives every Web Animation frame-by-frame; rAF loops, canvas, GIFs and video render as a frozen first frame.
  • Every duration must divide evenly into the loop period (--mo-loop 24s / --mo-loop-short 12s), or the loop visibly jumps. --verify catches this.
  • Size in cqh/cqw, never px, inside .stage__canvas. Minimum legible sizes are tokens: --type-min-venue (4cqh), --type-min-venue-support (2.2cqh).
Broadcast timing — a separate scale from UI
--mo-reveal / --mo-exit700ms / 500msentrances and exits
--mo-hold*3–8sthe stillness between events
--mo-loop / -short24s / 12sloop periods; all durations divide into these
--dur-fast/base/slow120–320msstay for interfaces only
Rendering
node render/rec.cjs --in motion/idle-loop.html \
  --size 1920x1080 --fps 30 --secs 24 --verify \
  --out dist/idle-loop.mp4
.mp4 → H.264 (venue, socials) · .mov → ProRes 422 HQ (editors, VJ rigs) · .webm → VP9 · --scale 2 renders 4K from a 1080 layout · --verify on anything that loops

07 · Templates
Next Generation cover

Camp teen-drama DVD-cover key art on the club navy field — badly-pasted cast cutouts with tape, season banner, review pull-quotes, ticket band. Ships as a reusable template with drop-in image slots; a designer fills the frames and exports.

Working with it
  • Location: templates/next-generation-cover/.
  • Formats: square 1080×1080 (feed) and story 1080×1920 — the story cut re-flows the cast grid to two columns.
  • Frame options: gold keyline or sequin grain — one frame, never both.
  • Image slots: six cast frames accept dropped photos; bad crops are encouraged — the paste job is the joke.
  • Cast names are stand-ins; open slots are written as cast reveals so the layout works before confirmation.
Rules in force: one Harbour lockup (the title), spectrum as a rule, gold carries the facts, marquee bulbs close the sheet. If a new template breaks one of these, it is not in the register.
